Data updates only when you load or reload the page (or hit Refresh) — nothing runs in the background here. From xmplaylist.com, a community tracker with a ~2 min cache; not affiliated with SiriusXM. To add stations, edit STATIONS in index.html and SLUGS in fetch_plays.py — the slug is the last part of any xmplaylist station URL.
